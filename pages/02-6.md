**Edition date** — 2026-10-12

· One-line conclusion: The headline "AI companies want 100B on-device chips" turns a Qualcomm CEO's podcast remark about **unnamed** AI companies' **2028** target into an industry-wide present-tense demand.
· Key fact 1: On October 8, 2026 (local time), Qualcomm CEO Cristiano Amon said on the *Sources* podcast that "some AI companies" want phones able to run **at least a 100-billion-parameter model by 2028**, running continuously — and he **declined to name them**.
· Key fact 2: No such chip exists today. Qualcomm's current flagship (Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6) runs roughly **30B-parameter MoE** models on-device — the 100B target is **more than triple** that scale.
· Key fact 3: The speaker is the **chip vendor's CEO**, speaking at his own chip-announcement cycle, with **no named customer, model, or demonstration** — so the claim is not independently verifiable.
