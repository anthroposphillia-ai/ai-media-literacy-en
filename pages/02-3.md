## Case 3. The headline that Chinese AI developers publicly disclosed safety tests for "just 3.6%" of

**Edition date** — 2026-10-10

· One-line conclusion: The headline that Chinese AI developers publicly disclosed safety tests for "just 3.6%" of releases measures *disclosure*, not safety — the report itself says the rest may have been tested privately, and it gives no comparable U.S. rate.
· Key fact 1: SemiAnalysis reviewed 857 model releases (2021–Sep 15, 2026) from nine Chinese developers; 31 (3.6%) had a model-matched public safety result, and only 9 (1.1%) had one by launch.
· Key fact 2: No disclosure was found for 813 releases, but the report notes companies "could have conducted tests privately" — this is a visibility metric, not proof of untested models.
· Key fact 3: The report provides no release-level U.S. comparison, so it cannot support a China-vs-U.S. ranking.
· Source: [Reuters, "China AI developers publish safety tests for just 3.6% of model releases, report finds"](https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/china-ai-developers-publish-safety-tests-just-36-model-releases-report-finds-2026-10-09/).
