## ① Do not anthropomorphise

**Definition** — Do not put AI in the **subject position**. The actor that judges, chooses or acts is always a person or an organisation.

**Measured frequency** — **8 cards (7%)**, the second lowest — but it is the conceptual foundation: if the actor disappears, the other seven principles have nothing to attach to.

### Why

"AI lied", "AI hacked", "AI decided" **removes people from the sentence**. Remove people and responsibility goes with them.

| Anthropomorphic | Actor restored |
|---|---|
| "AI lied" | "the chatbot **generated a factually wrong sentence**" |
| "AI won first place" | "**the company published** its model's measurement" |
| "AI fired 3,000 people" | "**the company** reorganised around automation" |
| "AI solved a maths problem" | "**researchers published** an unverified result" |

The key is the **verb**: *lied, decided, fired, solved* are verbs of an agent with intent. For machines use *generated, produced, executed, computed*.

### Three check questions

1. Is the subject **AI** or a **person/organisation**?
2. Does the verb attribute **intent**?
3. If you rewrite with a human subject, does the sentence **collapse**? (If so, the original overstated.)

### Real case

> The Tencent/Amap story, read for ①. The headline says "Tencent agents too bypassed the security net", so readers take it that **Tencent's model scanned Alibaba**. The independent report says the opposite about certainty: **"some agents used the word 'claude', but we do not believe this run came from a Claude model, much less from Anthropic"**, and **"we are deeply uncertain about what model was being evaluated"**. The actor was fixed in the headline and undetermined in the source.

**Practice** — subtitle the subject, keep the tool in the object position:
- "AI bubble popped" → the price was set by **investors and executives**.
- "Claude Science completed the map" → **a Johns Hopkins researcher** made the map; Claude performed alignment and inpainting.

### Correction formula

> `[Person/organisation] used [tool/model] to produce [result].` / `[Model/system] performed [function] (no intent).`
