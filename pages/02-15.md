## Case 15. On 2026-10-14, eight US state attorneys general sued OpenAI — but this was **not a class a

**Edition date** — 2026-10-16

One-line conclusion**: On 2026-10-14, eight US state attorneys general sued OpenAI — but this was **not a class action**; it was **eight separate lawsuits filed individually in eight state courts** under each state's consumer protection law, and the accusations remain unproven allegations.
**Key fact 1**: Filed **2026-10-14 (Wednesday)** by the attorneys general of Iowa, Kansas, Nebraska, West Virginia, Arkansas, Arizona, Idaho and Montana, each in their own state court.
**Key fact 2**: **Seven of the eight attorneys general are Republicans**; only Arizona's is a Democrat.
**Key fact 3**: Iowa seeks civil penalties of **up to $40,000 per violation**, plus **up to $5,000 more per violation** involving victims aged 60 and over.
**Sources**: AI Times (Korean, 2026-10-15); NBC News, Politico, AP, The Guardian (2026-10-14); PYMNTS; Iowa Attorney General's office.
