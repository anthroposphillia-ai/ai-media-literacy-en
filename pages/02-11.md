**Edition date** — 2026-10-14

· One-line conclusion: Two widely circulated stories dated October 13, 2026 — "Anthropic warns its AI could self-replicate on smart devices within months" and "AI found the melon yield gene in 4 minutes" — both rest on self-published evaluations by the parties that built the models, and both drop the conditions their own authors stated.
· Key fact 1: Anthropic's Frontier Red Team report (published October 12, 2026) shows end-to-end replication achieved by **GLM-5.2, a third-party open-weight model — not an Anthropic model**. Anthropic's own result was narrower: a 9B model got inference running on 8 of 9 hardware types.
· Key fact 2: The "months away" figure is conditional: "If small models improve at their historical rate, they'll match this capability level by the end of this year." The 17-node spread test ran with **ADB ports open without authentication**, and the authors state they "largely ignore the cyber exploit element."
· Key fact 3: The melon case is a **retrospective re-ranking of an already published** flower sex-determination study (gene *CmEIN3*), placed first among **3,061** candidates. In the vendor's own benchmark, the causal variant landed in the top 1% in only **48.8%** of cases.
