## ⑧ Offer alternatives

**Definition** — "The AI era is unstoppable" is **determinism**, not reporting. How far to adopt is a social choice, and coverage should show that choices exist.

**Measured frequency** — **14 cards (13%)**.

### Why

Deterministic sentences turn readers into an **audience**. "Already happening", "unstoppable tide", "on the RSI treadmill" erase options — and with them, responsibility. If there is no choice, nobody has to answer for anything.

Three signals:

1. **Tense manipulation** — "is being adopted" becomes "has been adopted"; a forecast becomes a fact.
2. **Actor deletion** — "adoption is spreading" with nobody deciding.
3. **No alternatives** — partial adoption, deferral, refusal and regulation go unmentioned.

### Three check questions

1. Who **decided** this — and who decided *not* to?
2. Are **partial adoption, deferral or refusal** cases in the piece?
3. Is there **one line** on what a reader can do (ask, question, supervise)?

### Real case

> 「Google co-founder Brin: AI entering a phase of self-improvement」 (AI Times, 2026-10-17) — [original](https://www.aitimes.com/news/articleView.html?idxno=216350)

**What is wrong** — "on the RSI (recursive self-improvement) treadmill" does three things. It **converts a forecast into a phase**: one executive's remark becomes an industry state. It **deletes branches**: in the same period companies were making different choices about deployment scope, evaluation and safety conditions, and regulators were attaching conditions. And it **makes the reader an audience**: if it is a phase, there is nothing to ask.

**Corrected**

> Google co-founder Sergey Brin **predicted** that AI is starting to get onto a self-improvement treadmill; his stated evidence was the short interval between model releases. This is **one executive's assessment**, and in the same period companies are choosing different deployment scopes, evaluation methods and safety conditions, while regulators attach conditions. **How far and how fast to adopt remains undecided.**

### Correction formula

> `[Forecast/claim] was made. However, [actors] can choose [full adoption / partial·deferral / refusal·regulation], and [examples] are currently under way. The change is therefore a decision, not a fixed future.`
