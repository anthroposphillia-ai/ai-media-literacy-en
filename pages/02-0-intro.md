## Case files — the citation block of every edition

Each edition of the daily audit ended with a copy-pasteable **citation block**: a one-line
verdict, three verified facts, and the sources that back them. They are reproduced here
unchanged (18 case files, 2026-10-09 → 2026-10-17).

Read them as worked examples of the principles: each verdict restores the actor, the
comparison set or the source grade that the original coverage dropped.
