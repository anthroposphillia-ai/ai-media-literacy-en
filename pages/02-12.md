## Case 12. Kroger's 3,000 corporate job cuts are **not** a case the company attributed to AI. The CEO

**Edition date** — 2026-10-14

One-line conclusion: Kroger's 3,000 corporate job cuts are **not** a case the company attributed to AI. The CEO memo never mentions AI, the cuts target the corporate office (not stores), and the wire headline supplied the causation.
Key facts ① (2026-10-12) Kroger CEO Greg Foran told employees the company will cut **about 3,000 corporate roles over three years**; his memo names "technology" but not "artificial intelligence."
Key facts ② The cuts equal about **37% of Kroger's ~8,200 corporate staff** and **0.7% of its 409,000 total employees** (per Gate News / Food Trade News); savings are described as reinvested in **store operations, not AI infrastructure**.
Key facts ③ Kroger's AI strategy was announced **2026-09-22** at GroceryShop; Chief Digital Officer Yael Cosset said **"Our goal is not to automate everywhere."** AP placed this and the non-AI drivers (competition, failed Albertsons merger, $600M fee suit) in the body and dropped them from the headline.
