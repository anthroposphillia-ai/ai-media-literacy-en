## Case 8. The "2.7× MoE training efficiency" headline describes **Ai2's own preliminary system test 

**Edition date** — 2026-10-13

· One-line conclusion: The "2.7× MoE training efficiency" headline describes **Ai2's own preliminary system test on eight NVIDIA B300 GPUs against its own earlier FSDP implementation** — not finished training speed, model quality, or cost savings.
· Key fact 1: Ai2 released the open-source MoE training framework **Olmo-core 3 on October 1, 2026**, switching from FSDP to DDP with expert parallelism (code: github.com/allenai/olmo-core).
· Key fact 2: Scaling experts from 8 to 128 with top-4 routing held active parameters near **3.2B** while total capacity grew from **4.6B to 47B**, with throughput falling **less than 5%**.
· Key fact 3: Ai2 labels the result a "**preliminary test**" and states the tests "used **random routing** to measure system performance, rather than the quality of a trained model." The 1.2T / 512-GPU figure (up to **858 TFLOP/s/GPU**) is a peak observed systems number.
