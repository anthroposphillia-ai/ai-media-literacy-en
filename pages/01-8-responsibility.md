## ② Name who is responsible

**Definition** — Not "the system did it", but **who decided to design, adopt, operate and supervise it**. A story with no responsible actor leaves readers no one to hold to account.

**Measured frequency** — **8 cards (7%)**, tied last — and the other foundation principle: without it, judgement stops at "something happened".

### Why

Responsibility in AI cases is **almost always split**. Coverage that names one branch, or collapses everything into "the system", hides that anyone was responsible at all.

| Seat | Question |
|---|---|
| Design | who decided to build this capability |
| Adoption | who bought it and budgeted for it |
| Operation | who granted access and directed the run |
| Supervision | who deployed knowing the risk |
| Response | who investigated and fixed it afterwards |

### Three check questions

1. Is any responsible party named **by name or title**?
2. Is a multi-branch responsibility collapsed into one branch?
3. If there is no legal liability, is **operational/design responsibility** still stated?

### Real case 1

> The Tencent/Amap story, read for ②. On the source's own account responsibility runs at least four ways: the evaluation team that ran the agents, the provider of the model under evaluation, **the party who exposed the Amap API key and bot tokens in a public repository** (absent from the article), and the infrastructure provider managing sandbox egress (also absent). Naming only the first two leaves the "rogue Chinese actors" frame intact and erases the technical mistake that made the run possible.

### Real case 2

> A headline reading **"AI companies asked Qualcomm"** reads as *several companies, confirmed*. The same article's body says **"it did not disclose which companies made the request"**, and the English original limits it to **"some AI companies"**. What is confirmed is that **one CEO said he heard such a request** — counting actors is part of naming responsibility.

### Real case 3

> Where the criminal provision defines the actor as a **person**, a system-generated filing may fall outside it: **no legal subject exists to punish**. Responsibility then sits with the operating organisation's evaluation design and reporting delay. **Absence of legal liability is not absence of responsibility.**

### Correction formula

> `Among [design / adoption / operation / supervision], the responsible party is [person/organisation]. Where no legal subject exists, operational and design responsibility sits with [party]; [unresolved items] remain undisclosed.`
