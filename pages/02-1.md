## Case 1. Anthropic's "first complete UV map of the sky" was not a new AI observation — it is an edu

**Edition date** — 2026-10-09

· One-line verdict: Anthropic's "first complete UV map of the sky" was not a new AI observation — it is an educational map assembled from public data, with roughly one-third of the sky filled in by a statistical model.
· Fact 1: About a third of the map (including much of the galactic plane, which GALEX avoided) is not measured but *inferred* via inpainting, correlating UV with visible, infrared and radio data.
· Fact 2: The author, Brice Ménard, is a Johns Hopkins astrophysicist *and* an Anthropic researcher; the announcement appeared on Anthropic's own blog (Oct 8, 2026), not a peer-reviewed paper.
· Fact 3: A human (Ménard) caught an artifact the AI agents' two review passes missed, prompting correction of 38,000 observations.
· Source: [Anthropic, "The missing map of the sky"](https://www.anthropic.com/research/the-missing-map-of-the-sky)
